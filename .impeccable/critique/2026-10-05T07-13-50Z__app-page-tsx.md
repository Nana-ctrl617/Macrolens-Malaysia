# MacroLens Malaysia review — 5 October 2026

MacroLens has a coherent compact presentation, but its accuracy safeguards and evidence pathways are not yet as strong as its presentation. Fix those before adding more sections.

## Design specificity and strengths

The visual system is specifically authored for Malaysian economic research: ringgit units, official observation periods, state comparisons, disciplined rust/teal accents and readable system sans. Keep this identity. Grouped navigation, locally scrolling tables, accessible forecast interval labels and educational boundaries are useful. Tested representative routes had no page-wide overflow at desktop/mobile dimensions. A decorative redesign is not indicated.

## Priority issues

### [P1] Accuracy and credibility labels

The live national mean household expenditure is RM5,363.4375, calculated as an unweighted mean of sixteen state means, rather than DOSM's 2024 national household mean of RM5,566. This affects national comparison fields in the API; the displayed KL/Sarawak income levels are not implicated. Fix: retrieve the official national spending series rather than averaging state means.

Evidence: pipeline/macrolens.py:572, 824; live /api/dashboard-v7; official https://storage.dosm.gov.my/hies/household_expenditure_2024.pdf.

OPR and MGS are stale, but Brief says Latest data included and several derived objects say fresh. Data Health displays Building because it expects overall/note while the payload contains overallHealth/summary; it also formats health through a risk-level label helper. The sensitivity overlay calls MacroLens output an official forecast. Fix: validate the complete payload contract, propagate all input statuses, distinguish attempt time from successful retrieval, and label observations versus generated analysis accurately.

Evidence: app/page.tsx:1147, 1766, 1795, 1868; app/lib/dashboard.ts:186, 282; pipeline/macrolens.py:1693, 1699, 1785, 1804, 1938, 1983.

Suggested commands: impeccable clarify / harden.

### [P1] Evidence navigation and accessibility

Live Open full diagnostics closes the modal and changes to /#structural but remains on Snapshot. Fix: navigate directly to /structural and preserve selected indicator. The indicator dialog does not move or trap focus; Tab reaches obscured background cards. Fix: initial focus, inert background, focus containment and restoration; include value/period/status in accessible KPI descriptions.

Evidence: app/page.tsx:394-404, 419-429, 473-480, 543, 1894-1899; live keyboard and route checks by A.

Risk's Overall screen label is 12px, foreground #c73b2d on #203530, contrast 2.54:1. Fix its foreground for the dark surface. Regional rows show visible keyboard focus and source notes, but the chart role=img hides individual values from the accessibility tree; its semantic table alternative works. Chart/Table buttons lack selected-state semantics.

Evidence: app/globals.css:218, 346; app/page.tsx:1667, 1672-1679; B measured live output. No real screen-reader certification was performed.

Suggested commands: impeccable audit / harden.

### [P1] News date, attribution and decoding defects

On 5 October the live News page promised Past seven days but included January, March, May and June 2026 stories. Duplicate same-story MarketWatch destinations appear through different Bing redirects. News source groups unrelated publishers. Text includes broken UTF-8/entity decoding. Fix: strict publication-window enforcement, explicit cached/archive modes, canonical URL deduplication, publisher attribution and one safe text-normalisation pass.

On mobile the first headline begins around y=1544 because three general-purpose images precede the feed. Fix: lead with current dated headlines; use photos only where they add relevant context.

Evidence: app/page.tsx:1219, 1238-1244; /news desktop/mobile observed by B. Article claims were not independently verified.

Suggested commands: impeccable harden / distill.

### [P2] Statistical precision exceeds shown validation

Risk 37.2 is an equal-weight average of nine hand-set rules, not a probability or empirically validated danger estimate. Some method text mentions percentile/change ingredients not used in the score itself. Fix: name it a descriptive monitoring index, expose its scale and faithful calculations, and justify or test thresholds.

Forecast selection uses twelve recent overlapping origins. ARIMAX RMSE 0.1782 versus SARIMA 0.1948 is a small advantage without a separate test period, horizon-specific scores or stability evidence. The intervals are nominal model-conditional bands, not empirically verified coverage. Fix: longer rolling evaluation, per-horizon error and interval coverage, honest calibration language.

No future-exogenous-row leakage was found: future rows are overwritten with origin inputs. Core through the origin matches the intended plan; the blanket one-month-lag wording is inaccurate.

Structural candidate p-values are conditional on dates selected in the same dataset; Holm over selected candidates does not account for the whole unknown-date search. Warnings are disclosed. Fix: stronger exploratory/conditional wording, break-date uncertainty and appropriate unknown-break inference rather than presenting the tests as independent confirmation.

Evidence: pipeline/macrolens.py:1425, 1450-1451, 1489, 1557, 1575-1582, 1591-1594, 1622-1629, 1762-1789. These are methodological limitations, not a claim that the model has demonstrated leakage.

Suggested commands: impeccable clarify plus a separate statistical validation task.

### [P2] Information inventory overwhelms prioritised explanation

Snapshot leads with six equally weighted levels and repeated card furniture, without changes or a ranked This month reading. Brief Why lists generic possible mechanisms rather than period-specific evidence. Nineteen destinations remain, including six Markets/research siblings and ambiguous Drivers/Growth drivers, BOP and Brief/Report.

Fix: keep the compact system; add measured changes and a short ranked reading, rename ambiguous routes, differentiate Brief/Report, and attach dated evidence or measured contributions to explanations. Do not invent causal attribution. Allow shareable selected region, indicator and date range.

Evidence: app/page.tsx:392-406, 1941-1950; app/components/DashboardNavigation.tsx:8-39; live /brief.

Suggested commands: impeccable layout / clarify / distill.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

Moderate load: chunking, minimal choices and working-memory continuity fail. Grouping, hierarchy and progressive disclosure are generally useful. Readers gain confidence from precise figures and interactive evidence, then lose it when stale inputs become reassuring summaries or a diagnostics exit fails. Keep the responsible disclaimer, but supply a clear next evidence step.

- Sam / keyboard and screen reader: modal focus behind overlay, KPI accessible names omit values/status. Regional table and forecast chart keyboard support are positives.

## Minor observations

- Mobile region selections truncate important meanings; several 38-41px controls miss a 44px ergonomic target. This is not a blanket WCAG 2.2 failure claim.
- No h1 on the five B-tested routes; sidebar h2 headings precede page titles.
- Schema-version messages are briefly shown during loading; source-confirmed error recovery can leave loading-looking null states without retry guidance.
- Household spending is not a pure regional price index. Median gross income minus mean spending is a disclosed rough proxy, not disposable cash or household savings.
- Old explorer geometry is less compact than current dashboard surfaces. Internal links, modal opening and external links share the same arrow affordance.
